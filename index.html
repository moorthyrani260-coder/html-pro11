Meet Our Speaker

Scott Sampson | February

Discover the amazing world of dinosaurs with Dr. Scott Sampson! Learn about a 75-million-year-old Utahceratops and explore the mysteries of prehistoric life at the Saroyan.

Scott Sampson is a Canadian-born paleontologist with a Ph.D. in zoology from the University of Toronto. His research has explored horned dinosaurs, their physical characteristics, and their development during the Late Cretaceous period.

After earning his doctorate, Sampson worked at the American Museum of Natural History in New York. He later taught anatomy at the New York College of Osteopathic Medicine before joining the University of Utah as a professor and curator of vertebrate paleontology.

His scientific interests include dinosaur evolution, anatomy, and relationships between species. His fieldwork has taken him to Africa, Madagascar, Canada, and the United States.

Beyond research, Sampson hosted the Discovery Channel series Dinosaur Planet. He also authored Dinosaur Odyssey: Fossil Threads in the Web of Life, a book about dinosaurs and the ecosystems they inhabited.
